08 — Questions

Frequently asked questions

Deposits, alcohol, smoking, age and how to book
Is there a deposit?

VIP rooms have a 15 000 ₽ deposit, the Premium room 30 000 ₽. The deposit covers your whole order — hookah, food, tea and drinks — except alcohol, which is paid separately. The large and small halls have no deposit.

Can I bring my own alcohol?

No, outside alcohol is not allowed.

Can I bring my pet?

Sorry, no — pets are not allowed.

Can I vape inside?

Yes, e-cigarettes and vapes are allowed indoors.

What about regular cigarettes?

Regular cigarettes only on the terrace. They are not allowed indoors.

What is the minimum age?

18+ only. We may ask for ID at the door.

How do I book a table?

Fill in the form on this page — it goes straight to our manager. Or message @dynastylounge on Telegram, or call +7 909 966-21-16.

Do you have a birthday discount?

Yes! 10% off for the birthday guest. See the terms in the Dynasty Club section.

What are your opening hours?

Daily from 12:00 to 06:00.
